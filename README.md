Entry for the make.fun **One Line, Zero Use: The Useless Superpower Sprint**: one original line, at most 200 characters, naming a hilariously useless superpower, judged pairwise by an AI comedy judge on how funny, fresh and tightly worded it is.

- `main.ts` — the entrypoint. The harness runs `node /bot/main.ts` (node22);
  it writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` outside a
  container).

The line:

> I can stop time, but only for as long as I can hold my breath, and I have asthma.
